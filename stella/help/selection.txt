SELECTION OPERATIONS

Stella uses one unified selection model for every selection method:

  WordStar block   Ctrl-B B (begin) ... Ctrl-B E (end)
  vi visual mode   v (character-wise) or V (line-wise) in vi mode
  Keyboard         move with the cursor while a block/visual mark is active
  Mouse            click to place the cursor, drag to select,
                   scroll wheel to scroll

Selected text is always highlighted (black on cyan on colour terminals,
reverse video on monochrome terminals).  Single-line and multi-line
selections are supported and update dynamically while dragging.

Block commands (Ctrl-B prefix, WordStar mode)
  Ctrl-B C  copy selection to clipboard
  Ctrl-B M  move selection to the cursor
  Ctrl-B D  delete selection
  Ctrl-B U  unmark (clear) selection

In vi visual mode: d deletes the selection, y copies it, Esc exits.
